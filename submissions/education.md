## 500-word narrative

Tournament-grade shuttlecocks are not cheap. A serious junior burns through $1,500 to $3,000 a year in shuttlecocks alone — before coaching, travel, or kit. For many families, that's the difference between their child playing badminton at a competitive level and quitting. A humidifier that cuts that bill by 67–89% isn't an environmental story to them. It's the story of whether their child gets to keep playing — and whether they can stay on a path that leads to scholarship-funded education.

I built the **HmBr Badminton Scholarship Fund** on top of that idea. The fund is hosted on Bold.org, a registered 501(c)(3) scholarship platform where every contribution is verifiable, tax-deductible, and protected by the Bold.org Foundation. Today the fund stands at **$595 raised toward a $50,000 goal across 19 individual contributors** — coaches, parents, players who believed in the cause. I show these real numbers prominently on hmbrimpact.org because the credibility of every claim downstream rests on judges being able to click through and verify them.

Why is the seed only $595? Because from 2020 through November 2025, *I personally was the fund.* I humidified tournaments and provided free humidification service to academies, players, and parents at my own expense — absorbing the operational cost out of pocket because the impact mattered more than the balance sheet at that moment. For five years, my time and money *were* the scholarship support, distributed in-kind rather than collected and disbursed. The arrangement was real but unscalable, and as my energy went into refining the product itself, the Bold.org fund could not grow alongside it.

Commercialization in late 2025 changes that math. The product is now generating ordinary sales and rewards-program activity, both of which feed the loop continuously. Every humidifier sold and every match played in the HmBr Match Challenge earns reward points that players can redeem for gear, tournament travel, or — and this is the path that matters for this category — direct scholarship funding. The scholarship loop is no longer dependent on one founder's bank account. **Players in India have already redeemed reward points for Yonex shoes**, demonstrating the loop is operating, not aspirational. Path 03 — Scholarship — funnels redeemed points into the Bold.org fund automatically.

The educational outcomes are direct. A junior athlete whose family saves $1,000 to $2,000 a year on shuttlecocks can route that savings into coaching, travel, or scholarship-eligible college preparation. A community whose tournament-organization costs drop reinvests the savings into youth programs and certification clinics. And every contributor to the Bold.org fund — whether through reward conversion or direct support — gets a 100% tax-deductible receipt and zero platform fees, because the entire fee structure is absorbed by the Bold.org Foundation.

The fund is small today and growing. The methodology is transparent. The path is open.
